# Remote Work Policy

**Last updated:** 2025-06-01
**Platform:** BookStack — HR Space > Policies
**Owner:** People Operations

## Overview

Employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to **2 days per week**. Remote days must be agreed upon with your direct manager in advance and should remain consistent week to week where possible.

## Eligibility

Remote work eligibility is determined by role type. Roles requiring on-site equipment, in-person client interaction, or physical presence for operational reasons are not eligible.

## Core Hours

Regardless of remote or in-office status, all employees are expected to be reachable during core hours of 10:00 AM–4:00 PM local time.
