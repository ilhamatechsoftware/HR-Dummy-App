# Annual Leave Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-07-10
**Platform:** BookStack — HR Space > Policies
**Owner:** People Operations

## Overview

All full-time employees are entitled to **18 days of annual leave** per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Annual leave is separate from sick leave and must be requested at least 5 business days in advance.

## Requesting Annual Leave

Submit requests through the HR portal. Requests longer than 5 consecutive days require two weeks' advance notice to allow for coverage planning.
