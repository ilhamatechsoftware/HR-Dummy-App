# Office Pet Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-18
**Owner:** Facilities

## Overview

Employees may bring well-behaved pets to the office **any day of the week**. This applies to both dogs and cats, provided they are up to date on vaccinations.

## Weight \& Size Limit

Pets must weigh **25 kg (55 lbs) or under** to be permitted in shared office spaces.

## Rules

* Pets must remain leashed or contained (e.g. carrier) in common areas.
* Pet owners are responsible for cleaning up after their pet.
* Pets are not permitted in the kitchen or food prep areas.
